Every few months a new model leapfrogs the last one. If your product's edge is "we use the best model," your edge lasts about one quarter.

What lasts longer is owning the place where the work already happens. The team that sits inside the spreadsheet, the inbox, or the CRM gets to swap in better models as they arrive, while everyone else has to convince users to switch.

That suggests a simple test for AI startups: **if a better model came out tomorrow, would it help you or help your competitor more?**

I don't think this holds forever. At some point, products built natively around AI will feel so different that distribution will matter less. But for now, I'd bet on workflow over raw capability.
