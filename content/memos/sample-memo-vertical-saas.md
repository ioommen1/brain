## Why this could work

1. **High switching costs.** Once patient records and billing live in the system, clinics rarely move.
2. **Payments attach.** Embedding payments can add meaningful revenue on top of software fees.
3. **Fragmented competition.** No national player with a modern product.

## Key risks

- A large incumbent bundles a "good enough" alternative
- Payments take rate compresses as volume grows
- Execution risk on moving from one product to several
