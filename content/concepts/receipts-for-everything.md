## The problem

Every purchase creates a little bundle of information: what you bought, when, for how much, the warranty, the return window. Today that information is scattered across email, paper, and a dozen retailer apps. When you actually need it (a return, an insurance claim, a broken appliance) it is gone.

## Who feels it most

Households that buy a lot of durable goods, freelancers who need receipts for expenses, and anyone who has missed a return window by two days.

## The concept

Forward your receipts (or connect your inbox) and get back a clean, searchable timeline of everything you own.

- Automatic extraction of item, price, merchant, and warranty terms
- Reminders before return windows and warranties expire
- One-tap export for taxes, expense reports, or insurance

## The wedge

Start with **return-window reminders**. It is a narrow, obvious, and immediately valuable job. Everything else builds on the data that feature collects.

## Why now

Language models make messy receipt parsing cheap and accurate enough to be invisible to the user.

## Open questions

> Is this a standalone product, or a feature that banks and card issuers will give away for free?

I lean toward thinking the data layer is the real business, and the consumer app is how you earn it.
