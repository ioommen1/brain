## The problem

Small teams run on memory and good intentions. Decisions get made in calls and forgotten. Nobody writes the weekly update. Follow-ups slip.

## The concept

An agent that sits across a team's calendar, docs, and chat and quietly does the work a great chief of staff would:

- Drafts the Monday priorities and the Friday recap
- Tracks commitments made in meetings and nudges owners
- Prepares a one-page brief before every external call

## What has to be true

The agent has to be trusted with access, and it has to be right often enough that people stop double-checking it. That makes **accuracy on a narrow set of tasks** more important than breadth.

## Risks

Incumbent workspace tools could ship this as a feature. The defense is depth: owning the operating cadence, not just the summaries.
